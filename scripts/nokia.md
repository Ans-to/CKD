SCENE 2 — FROM A SMALL COMPANY TO A GLOBAL GIANT

**Time:** 0:35–1:40

**[VOICEOVER]**

Nokia's story began long before smartphones existed.

Founded in Finland in 1865, the company originally operated as a paper mill.

Over time, Nokia expanded into different industries, including rubber, cables, and electronics.

But its biggest transformation came when telecommunications became one of the most important technologies in the world.

During the 1990s and early 2000s, mobile phones began changing how people communicated.

Nokia was perfectly positioned to take advantage of that shift.

By 2007, Nokia was the world's leading mobile phone manufacturer by shipments.

But success can create a dangerous illusion.

The belief that what made you successful yesterday will keep you successful tomorrow.
