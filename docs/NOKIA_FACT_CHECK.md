# Nokia documentary fact-check notes

Use primary or reputable sources for dates and numeric claims. These notes are source references for the Blender-only documentary; no source images or footage are imported.

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Nokia began as a single paper-mill operation in 1865; the business later spanned rubber, cable, electronics, and telecommunications. | Nokia, “Our history”: https://www.nokia.com/about-us/who-we-are/our-history/ |
| The first GSM call using Nokia equipment was made in 1991; Nokia became the world's best-selling mobile-phone brand by 1998. | Nokia, “Our history”: https://www.nokia.com/about-us/who-we-are/our-history/ |
| Nokia estimated about 40% global mobile-device market share in Q4 2007 and reported 437 million device volumes for 2007. | Nokia Q4 2007 earnings release: https://www.nokia.com/system/files/files/q4-2007-earnings-release-pdf.pdf |
| Nokia smartphone-market share estimates: 49.4% (2007), 43.7% (2008), 41.1% (2009), 34.2% (2010), and about 3% in H1 2013. These are Gartner estimates as summarized by BBC, and are not total handset market share. | BBC, “Nokia: The rise and fall of a mobile giant”: https://www.bbc.com/news/technology-23947212 |
| Nokia's 2011 strategic partnership with Microsoft was a response to competitive pressure from iOS and Android. | Nokia, “Our history”: https://www.nokia.com/about-us/who-we-are/our-history/ ; Nokia/Microsoft open letter (archived): https://web.archive.org/web/20110211110239/http://conversations.nokia.com/2011/02/11/open-letter-from-ceo-stephen-elop-nokia-and-ceo-steve-ballmer-microsoft/ |
| Microsoft announced a €5.44 billion transaction for substantially all of Nokia's Devices & Services business and patent licensing on 3 September 2013; the sale closed on 25 April 2014. | Microsoft announcement: https://news.microsoft.com/source/2013/09/03/microsoft-to-acquire-nokias-devices-services-business-license-nokias-patents-and-mapping-services/ ; Nokia completion release: https://www.nokia.com/newsroom/nokia-completes-sale-of-substantially-all-of-its-devices--services-business-to-microsoft/ |
| Nokia's current business is centered on network infrastructure, software, and technology licensing; the post-2013 company was not simply “gone.” | Nokia, “Our history”: https://www.nokia.com/about-us/who-we-are/our-history/ ; Nokia Annual Report 2024: https://www.nokia.com/system/files/2025-04/nokia-annual-report-2024_1.pdf |
| Apple's first iPhone was announced on 9 January 2007. | IEEE Spectrum, “How Nokia’s Feature Phones Lost to the Smartphone Era”: https://spectrum.ieee.org/nokia-phones-history |

Editorial safeguards: distinguish all-phone market share from smartphone share; attribute analyst estimates; label years on charts; describe strategic choices as contributors rather than a single-cause explanation; avoid invented internal conversations or claims about individual motives.
